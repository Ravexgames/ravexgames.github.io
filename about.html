About Ravex

Ravex is a game studio based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India, registered as a Micro enterprise under the Government of India’s Udyam scheme. It is operated by Rajveer Singh Nathawat as a sole proprietor. We make mobile games.

Who runs Ravex

Rajveer Singh Nathawat is the founder and game developer of Ravex. As a BCA graduate with three years of experience in the industry, he blends technical expertise with a passion for gaming. He uses this foundation in computer applications to design, code and continuously improve engaging mobile experiences.
